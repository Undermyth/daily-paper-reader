## 摘要
我们将经典的联想记忆 Hopfield 模型（其存储单个自旋的经典态）推广到存储纠缠量子态。该构造基于截断的投影算符之和哈密顿量，并且我们明确考虑了一个特殊情形：存储自旋-1/2系统的二聚体单重态覆盖。我们分析了所存储记忆的稳定性，发现容量——即稳定模式的数量——随系统规模的缩放速度快于根据与标准 Hopfield 模型的朴素类比所预期的结果，这表明可能存在量子优势。

## Abstract
We generalize the canonical Hopfield model of associative memory, which stores classical states of individual spins, to store entangled quantum states. The construction is based on truncated projector-sum Hamiltonian, and we explicitly consider a special case that stores dimer singlet coverings of a spin-1/2 system. We analyze the stability of the stored memories and find that the capacity -- the number of stable patterns -- scales faster with the system size than one would expect from a naive analogy with the standard Hopfield model, suggesting a possible quantum advantage.
