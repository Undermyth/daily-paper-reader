## 摘要
KV 缓存驱逐方法（如 SnapKV 和 PyramidKV）仅依据小观察窗口上的平均注意力对 token 排序。我们研究一个统一评分 $\mu_i+\lambda_1\sigma_i+\lambda_2\mathrm{corr}(i,S)$，其中加入了窗口查询间的注意力离散度以及相对于已选 token 的冗余度。当 $\lambda_2<0$ 时，该评分会像最大边际相关性（MMR）那样惩罚与已选 token 的相似性，且无需额外前向传播。为检验这种相关性-多样性平衡是否应随深度变化，我们将固定全局系数与三段式和二次型剖面进行比较。仅这些深度剖面在开发集上以 $\sinh$ 重参数化进行搜索。在 Mistral-7B 上、每层预算为 64 个条目时，在全部 16 个英文 LongBench 数据集上，单个全局多样化常数在 16 个数据集中有 13 个获得提升（宏平均 +1.1）；该增益在预算 32 时仍成立，在 128 时收窄。逐数据集搜索在大多数数据集上未发现可检测的层结构；在段落检索上发现了显著结构：一个中间层的符号翻转，奖励相似性，在预算 64 时相对基线提升 +9.6，且在无需重新调参的情况下，在预算 128 时相对全局常数提升 +13.2。消融实验将增益归因于冗余项；在留出测试集上重放每个被接受的搜索状态，可将真实结构与调参噪声区分开。

## Abstract
KV cache eviction methods such as SnapKV and PyramidKV rank tokens solely by mean attention over a small observation window. We study a unified score, $μ_i+λ_1σ_i+λ_2\mathrm{corr}(i,S)$, adding attention dispersion across window queries and redundancy relative to selected tokens. For $λ_2<0$, the score penalizes similarity to selected tokens as in maximal marginal relevance (MMR), without extra forward passes. To test whether this relevance-diversity balance should vary with depth, we compare fixed global coefficients with three-segment and quadratic profiles. Only these depth profiles are searched on a development split under a $\sinh$ reparameterization. On all 16 English LongBench datasets with Mistral-7B at a budget of 64 entries per layer, a single global diversification constant improves 13 of 16 datasets (macro +1.1); the gain holds at budget 32 and narrows at 128. Per-dataset search finds no detectable layer structure on most datasets; on passage retrieval it finds a large one: a mid-layer sign flip that rewards similarity and is worth +9.6 over the baseline at budget 64 and, without re-tuning, +13.2 over the global constant at budget 128. Ablations attribute the gain to the redundancy term; replaying every accepted search state on the held-out test set separates genuine structure from tuning noise.
