## 摘要
视觉假体可以通过动态模式化刺激产生简单的知觉，例如字母形状，然而尽管硬件完全能够进行高分辨率的时间控制，自然的动态视觉仍然遥不可及。这一持续存在的差距表明，瓶颈在于皮层本身如何表征人工输入。一个基本问题仍未解决：当人工视觉绕过自然的视网膜编码时，皮层表征中丢失了什么？在本文中，我们使用视网膜光遗传学小鼠模型来解决这个问题，该模型隔离了绕过视网膜编码的后果，同时保留了下游通路。通过对相同动态刺激下的受试者内V1皮层电生理和基于CNN的解码，我们揭示了可分离的、维度特异的差距。在时间上，人工视觉绕过了自然视觉所施加的频率依赖性压缩，扩展了刺激锁定的夹带带宽。在空间上，方向选择性表征经历了系统性重映射，伴随相位组织扰乱和编码规律性降低。这些发现表明，绕过自然视网膜编码会沿着可分离的时空维度重组皮层表征，建立了一个使得这种结构差异易于定位和量化的受试者内框架。

## Abstract
Visual prostheses can elicit simple percepts such as letter forms through dynamically patterned stimulation, yet naturalistic dynamic vision remains out of reach despite hardware fully capable of high-resolution temporal control. This persistent gap suggests the bottleneck lies in how artificial input is represented by the cortex itself. A fundamental question remains unresolved: when artificial vision bypasses natural retinal encoding, what is lost in cortical representation? We address this question in this paper using a retinal optogenetic mouse model that isolates the consequence of bypassing retinal encoding while preserving downstream pathways. Using within-subject V1 cortex electrophysiology and CNN-based decoding under identical dynamic stimuli, we reveal dissociable, dimension-specific gaps. Temporally, artificial vision bypassed the frequency-dependent compression imposed by natural vision, extending stimulus-locked entrainment bandwidth. Spatially, direction-selective representations underwent systematic remapping, with disrupted phase organization and reduced encoding regularity. These findings show that bypassing natural retinal encoding reorganizes cortical representation along dissociable spatiotemporal dimensions, establishing a within-subject framework that renders such structural divergences easy to locate and quantifiable.
