## 摘要
空间导航在阿尔茨海默病（AD）早期即受损，但AD病理所影响的神经计算仍不清楚。我们将虚拟现实导航与功能磁共振成像结合，在老年对照组、临床前AD和早期AD参与者中，将导航活动与脑脊液生物标志物及遗传风险相关联。早期AD与可分离的改变相关：记忆需求型导航期间与β-淀粉样蛋白相关的海马过度活跃、内嗅皮层附近与tau蛋白相关的六方向调节降低，以及后扣带回和内嗅皮层邻近区域以物体为中心的表征改变。以物体为中心的变化与参与者编码和检索目标位置的方式相关。海马过度活跃是最早、最显著的影响，并且是唯一区分临床前个体与对照组的功能测量指标。这些发现将AD生物标志物与人类不同的空间计算联系起来，并揭示了在明显痴呆之前基于导航的神经功能障碍功能特征。

## Abstract
Spatial navigation is impaired early in Alzheimer's disease (AD), but the neural computations affected by AD pathology remain unclear. We combined virtual-reality navigation with fMRI in aged controls, preclinical AD, and early AD participants, relating navigation activity to cerebrospinal fluid biomarkers and genetic risk. Early AD was associated with dissociable alterations: amyloid-$\beta$-linked hippocampal hyperactivity during memory-demanding navigation, tau-linked reductions in hexadirectional modulation near the entorhinal cortex, and altered object-centered representations in posterior cingulate and entorhinal-adjacent regions. Object-centered changes related to how participants encoded and retrieved target locations. Hippocampal hyperactivity was the earliest, most robust effect and the only functional measure distinguishing preclinical individuals from controls. These findings connect AD biomarkers to distinct spatial computations in humans and reveal navigation-based functional signatures of neural dysfunction before overt dementia.
