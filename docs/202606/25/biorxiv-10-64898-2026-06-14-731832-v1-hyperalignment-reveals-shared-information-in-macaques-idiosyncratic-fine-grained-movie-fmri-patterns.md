## 摘要
我们利用12只恒河猴在观看电影和反应超对齐（RHA）期间获得的fMRI数据，对猕猴大脑中的共享表征空间进行了建模。RHA显著提高了枕叶、颞叶和前额叶皮层中电影响应时间序列的个体间相关性，并实现了电影时间点的高精度个体间解码以及特异性功能拓扑的估计。这些结果揭示了在特异性的细粒度和粗粒度功能拓扑中编码的共享高维、高容量表征空间。

## Abstract
We modeled shared representational spaces in macaque brains using fMRI data from 12 rhesus monkeys obtained during movie-viewing and response hyperalignment (RHA). RHA greatly improved inter-subject correlation of movie response time-series in occipital, temporal, and prefrontal cortices and afforded highly accurate between-subject decoding of movie time-points and estimation of idiosyncratic functional topographies. These results reveal shared high-dimensional, high capacity representational spaces encoded in idiosyncratic fine- and coarse-grained functional topographies.
