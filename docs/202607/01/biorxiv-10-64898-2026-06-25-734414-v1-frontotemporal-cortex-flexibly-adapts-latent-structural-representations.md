## 摘要
人类通过形成抽象的、可在环境变化时灵活更新的结构表征，在复杂环境中导航表现出色。在此，我们研究了大脑如何动态重新配置这些内部模型以响应潜在层级中的隐蔽变化。通过一项新颖的推理任务和fMRI重复抑制，我们发现稳定的关系知识编码在眶额内侧皮层（mOFC），而结构变化则触发海马和前额叶区域的瞬时表征。新推断的关联首先在前内侧额叶皮层（amFC）编码，并在稳定后向腹侧迁移至mOFC。相反，过时的关联瞬时激活额极皮层和海马，而海马（而非额叶区域）保留残余记忆痕迹。值得注意的是，amFC和海马中早期新信号的强度追踪了行为适应的个体差异。总之，这些结果刻画了支持人类大脑适应性结构重组的机制，并对精神疾病中的认知灵活性障碍具有启示意义。

意义声明大脑依赖于隐藏环境结构的内部模型，然而当世界变化时这些模型如何被修订仍不清楚。我们开发了一项新颖的任务和神经成像方法，使我们能够追踪人类潜在结构表征的出现、维持和消失。稳定的表征编码在内侧眶额皮层，而新形成的和过时的关联在额叶和海马区域沿着不同的时间轨迹进行。过时的表征瞬时募集额极皮层，但在行为上不再相关后仍持续存在于海马中。更新的神经信号预测了适应的个体差异，揭示了认知灵活性及其在精神疾病中受损可能产生的候选机制。

## Abstract
Humans excel at navigating complex environments by forming abstract structural representations that can be flexibly updated when environments change. Here, we examine how the brain dynamically reconfigures these internal models in response to covert changes in latent hierarchies. Using a novel inference task and fMRI repetition suppression, we find that stable relational knowledge is encoded in medial orbitofrontal cortex (mOFC), while structural changes trigger transient representations across hippocampal and prefrontal regions. Newly inferred associations are first encoded in anterior medial frontal cortex (amFC) and migrate ventrally to mOFC when settling. In contrast, outdated associations transiently engage frontopolar cortex and hippocampus, with the hippocampus, but not frontal areas, retaining a residual memory trace. Notably, the strength of early novel signals in amFC and hippocampus tracks individual differences in behavioural adaptation. Together, these results characterise mechanisms supporting adaptive structural reconfiguration in the human brain, with implications for cognitive inflexibility in psychiatric disorders.

SIGNIFICANCE STATEMENTThe brain relies on internal models of hidden environmental structure, yet how these models are revised when the world changes remains unclear. We developed a novel task and neuroimaging approach that allowed us to trace the emergence, maintenance, and dissolution of latent structural representations in humans. Stable representations were encoded in medial orbitofrontal cortex, whereas newly formed and outdated associations followed distinct temporal trajectories across frontal and hippocampal regions. Outdated representations transiently recruited frontopolar cortex but persisted in hippocampus after becoming behaviorally irrelevant. Neural signatures of updating predicted individual differences in adaptation, revealing candidate mechanisms through which cognitive flexibility, and its impairment in psychiatric disorders, may arise.

## 7. 优点

- **时间分辨率**：通过SB块的顺序设计（SB-1, SB+1, SB+2）追踪表征的动态演化，揭示了amFC→mOFC的迁移。
- **结合个体差异**：将神经信号与行为恢复速度关联，增强了发现的生态效度和临床转化潜力。
- **临床意义**：明确指出了认知灵活性障碍（如OCD）中可能的神经异常节点（amFC和海马对新结构的响应不足）。

## 8. 不足与局限
