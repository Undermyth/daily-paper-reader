## 摘要
阿尔茨海默病以进行性记忆衰退为特征，但早期阶段海马表征如何变化仍 largely 未知。本研究利用现实世界头部固定空间交替任务结合在体双光子成像，探究5xFAD小鼠海马CA1表征。尽管7-9月龄5xFAD小鼠在任务学习中表现出显著缺陷，但2-4月龄小鼠表现正常，从而能够在行为衰退前评估海马编码。在此早期阶段，CA1神经元整体空间编码完整，但任务相关位置细胞表征受损。轨迹依赖编码揭示的情境表征在群体和单细胞水平均减弱，伴随行为选择期间从主要回顾性编码向更多前瞻性编码的显著转变。在任务学习过程中，长期不稳定的位置细胞空间编码受损更严重，而稳定的位置细胞在早期AD小鼠中基本保留。此外，化学遗传激活基底前脑胆碱能神经元增加了空间信息和会话内稳定性，并促进了这些早期5xFAD小鼠的轨迹依赖编码。总之，这些发现揭示了AD中与任务相关的海马空间和情境表征的选择性早期损伤，这些损伤先于空间交替行为表现的明显缺陷，并且仍可受胆碱能调节。

## Abstract
Alzheimer's disease (AD) is characterized by progressive memory decline, yet how hippocampal representations change at early AD stages remains largely unknown. In this study, we utilized a real-world head-fixed spatial alternation task combined with in vivo two-photon imaging to investigate hippocampal CA1 representations in 5xFAD mice. While 7-9 month old 5xFAD mice showed significant deficits in task learning, 2-4 month old mice performed normally, enabling assessment of hippocampal coding prior to behavioral decline. At this early stage, CA1 neurons exhibited intact global spatial encoding but demonstrated impaired task-related place cell representations. Contextual representations revealed by trajectory-dependent coding were weakened at both the population and single cell levels, accompanied by a notable shift from predominantly retrospective to more prospective encoding during behavioral choices. During task learning, longitudinally unstable place cells showed greater impairments in spatial coding, whereas stable place cells remained largely preserved in early-stage AD mice. Additionally, chemogenetic activation of basal forebrain cholinergic neurons increased spatial information and within-session stability, while promoting trajectory-dependent coding in these early-stage 5xFAD mice. Together, these findings reveal selective early impairments in hippocampal task-related spatial and contextual representations in AD that precede overt deficits in spatial alternation performance and remains amenable to cholinergic modulation.
