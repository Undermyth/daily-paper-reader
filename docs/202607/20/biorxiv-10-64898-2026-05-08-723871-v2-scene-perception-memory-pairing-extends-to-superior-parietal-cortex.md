## 摘要
视觉场景分析依赖于后大脑皮层的一组场景选择区域（OPA、PPA、MPA），其对应的记忆响应配对区域位于前部（LPMA、VPMA）或重叠皮层（MPMA）。这些配对区域之间的相互作用被认为将视觉输入与记忆背景整合起来。最近，在顶上小叶皮层中发现了第四个场景感知区域（SPA），其被认为在视觉引导导航中发挥作用。该区域是否也存在一个前部配对记忆区域目前尚不清楚。通过两个独立的fMRI数据集（总N=24，14名女性），使用静态或动态刺激以及不同的记忆任务，我们表明回忆视觉场景会在紧邻SPA前背侧的一个区域（此处称为SPMA）中引发强烈响应。在静息态fMRI中，SPA优先与其他场景感知区域耦合，而SPMA优先与其他位置记忆区域耦合。在全脑水平上，基于种子的连接性显示SPA位于四个处理流的交汇处，这些流涉及自体中心场景感知、基于地图的导航、视角采择和目标导向运动。这些发现将与视觉场景处理相关的感知-记忆主题扩展到第四个皮层表面。场景感知与记忆过程之间广泛的解剖耦合反映了这种相互作用对于灵活、基于上下文导航的重要性。

## Abstract
Visual scene analysis relies on a set of scene-selective regions in posterior cerebral cortex (OPA, PPA, MPA), with a paired memory-responsive counterpart located in anterior (LPMA, VPMA) or overlapping cortex (MPMA). The interaction between these pairs of regions is thought to integrate visual input with mnemonic context. Recently, a fourth scene-perception area in superior parietal cortex (SPA) was identified, with a proposed role in visually-guided navigation. Whether this region also has an anterior paired memory region is currently unknown. Across two independent fMRI datasets (total N=24, 14 females) using static or dynamic stimuli and distinct memory tasks, we show that recalling visual scenes evokes robust responses in a region (referred to here as SPMA) immediately anterior and dorsal to SPA. During resting-state fMRI, SPA preferentially coupled with the other scene-perception areas, while SPMA preferentially coupled with the other place memory areas. At the whole-brain level, seed-based connectivity revealed that SPA sits at the confluence of four processing streams spanning regions implicated in egocentric scene perception, map-based navigation, perspective taking, and goal-directed movement. These findings extend the perception-memory motif associated with visual scene processing to the fourth cortical surface. The widespread anatomical coupling between scene-perception and memory processes reflects the importance of this interaction for flexible, context-grounded navigation.
