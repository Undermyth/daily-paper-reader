## 摘要
对群体编码的完整理解需要连接神经处理的多个层面：单个神经元反应、群体表征和行为。我们通过将神经元调谐特性的分布与群体的表征几何及其对感知任务的效率联系起来，来连接这些层面。我们运用理论、对猕猴初级视觉皮层（V1）记录的分析以及模拟，揭示了调谐幅度和带宽的多样性如何增强视觉辨别和识别的群体编码。这两种类型的多样性驱动了表征几何的不同但互补的变化。幅度多样性增加了对不同刺激的反应之间的欧氏距离，而带宽多样性则在它们之间创造了更大的角度距离。前者利用了神经元可用的发放率范围，后者则利用了群体反应的高维特性。群体编码可以通过这两种不同的几何变化得到改善，而幅度和带宽多样性为实现这一点提供了生物学机制。

亮点
• 反应幅度多样性和调谐带宽多样性的增加都能改善感知表现。
• 两种多样性均能改善视觉辨别和识别。
• 幅度多样性对辨别的改善更大，而带宽多样性对识别的改善更大。
• 表征几何揭示了这些效应背后的机制。

## Abstract
A complete understanding of population coding requires connecting multiple levels of neural processing: individual responses, population representations, and behavior. We link these by relating the distribution of neuronal tuning properties to a populations representational geometry and its efficiency for perceptual tasks. We use theory, analysis of recordings from macaque primary visual cortex (V1), and simulations to reveal how diversity of tuning amplitude and bandwidth enhances the population code for visual discrimination and identification. Both types of diversity drive different, but complementary changes to the representational geometry. Amplitude diversity increases the Euclidean distance between the responses to different stimuli, while bandwidth diversity creates a larger angular distance between them. The first utilizes the range of firing rates available to neurons, and the second exploits the high-dimensional nature of population responses. Population codes can be improved using these two different geometric changes, and amplitude and bandwidth diversity provide biological mechanisms for doing so.
