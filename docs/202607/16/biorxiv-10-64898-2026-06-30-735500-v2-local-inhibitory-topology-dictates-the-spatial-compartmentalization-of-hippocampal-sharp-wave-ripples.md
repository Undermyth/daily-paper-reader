## 摘要
海马尖波涟漪（SWRs）对记忆巩固至关重要，并且是大脑中最同步的振荡事件之一。然而，尽管它们具有广泛同步的能力，SWRs 常常仍局限于离散的海马区域，揭示了全局协调与局部自主性之间的悖论。在此，通过分析现有体内记录并结合实验约束的三维生物物理模型，我们表明抑制性活动和抑制性拓扑起着根本不同的作用。体周抑制控制 SWRs 的产生，而树突抑制调节波纹振荡的强度和频谱特性，同时抑制性连接的空间组织建立了局部计算域，使自主波纹发生器能够共存。总之，我们的发现揭示了抑制的空间维度，其中抑制性活动控制 SWRs 的出现和动态，而抑制性拓扑决定其空间组织和自主性。

## Abstract
Hippocampal sharp-wave ripples (SWRs) are essential for memory consolidation and represent among the most synchronous oscillatory events in the brain. Yet, despite their capacity for widespread synchronization, SWRs frequently remain confined to discrete hippocampal domains, revealing a paradox between global coordination and local autonomy. Here, by analysing existing in vivo recordings with an experimentally constrained three-dimensional biophysical model, we show that inhibitory activity and inhibitory topology serve fundamentally distinct functions. Whereas perisomatic inhibition gates SWR generation and dendritic inhibition regulates the strength and spectral properties of ripple oscillations, the spatial organization of inhibitory connectivity establishes local computational domains that enable autonomous ripple generators to coexist. Together, our findings identify a spatial dimension of inhibition, in which inhibitory activity governs the emergence and dynamics of SWRs, while inhibitory topology determines their spatial organization and autonomy.

3.  **层次化的控制机制：**

    *   **二级（树突抑制）→ 幅度/功率调节。**

*   **缺乏动态可塑性：** 模型中的连接权重是静态的（除了输入驱动），未考虑突触可塑性（如STDP）可能如何随时间重塑这些“空间域”。学习过程中的动态变化是一个重要的研究方向。
*   **实验条件的理想化：** 尽管设计了异质性输入，但仍相对理想化。真实的体内驱动环境可能更为多变和复杂。
