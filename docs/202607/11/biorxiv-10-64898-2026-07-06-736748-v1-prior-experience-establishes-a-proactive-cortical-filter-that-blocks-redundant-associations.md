## 摘要
先前的经验通过过滤掉多余的线索来允许选择性学习，阻断效应就是一个例子：先前对某一线索的学习会阻止在随后的复合条件作用中学习一个新的但冗余的线索。自上而下的皮层-皮层通路被认为参与感觉过滤，但它们在阻断效应中是否、如何以及何时发挥作用仍不清楚。在此，我们在小鼠中建立了一个头部固定的阻断范式，其中先前的听觉线索-奖赏学习阻止了在随后的复合条件作用中建立冗余的体感线索-奖赏关联。我们发现，从前扣带回皮层（ACC）到初级体感皮层（S1）的投射促进了阻断。先前的听觉学习在明确的体感经验之前，通过小清蛋白神经元建立了ACC驱动的对S1的抑制性控制，从而抑制了对体感线索的爆发响应，限制了网络可塑性，并促进了阻断。这些发现提出了一种主动的皮层过滤器，它预先配置了抑制性的感觉偏差，以限制未来对冗余线索的学习。

## Abstract
Prior experience allows selective learning by filtering out redundant cues, as exemplified by blocking, in which prior learning about one cue prevents a novel but redundant cue from being learned during subsequent compound conditioning. Top-down cortico-cortical pathways have been implicated in sensory filtering, but whether, how, and when they contribute to blocking remain unknown. Here we establish a head-fixed blocking paradigm in mice in which prior auditory cue-reward learning prevents a redundant somatosensory cue-reward association during subsequent compound conditioning. We found that a projection from anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) to primary somatosensory cortex (S1) facilitates blocking. Prior auditory learning establishes ACC-driven inhibitory control over S1 via parvalbumin neurons, before explicit somatosensory experience, thereby suppressing burst responses to the somatosensory cue, limiting network plasticity, and promoting blocking. These findings propose a proactive cortical filter that preconfigures inhibitory sensory bias to constrain future learning about redundant cues.
