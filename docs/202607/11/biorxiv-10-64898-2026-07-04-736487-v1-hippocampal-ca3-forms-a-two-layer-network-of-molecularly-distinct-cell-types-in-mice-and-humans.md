## 摘要
联想记忆关键依赖于海马系统核心的CA3回返回路。尽管CA3锥体神经元（PNs）的多样性早有报道，但组织细胞类型身份的原则及其对回路功能的影响仍不清楚。在这里，我们证明深层-浅层是CA3异质性的主要轴。PNs分为两种分子不同的亚型：浅层St18-神经元和深层St18+神经元，它们具有不同的解剖、分子和功能特性。利用St18-Cre小鼠品系，我们揭示这些亚型具有不同的连接模式。浅层神经元形成跨半球的典型广泛自联想网络，并投射到CA1，而深层St18+ PNs则形成一个显著不同的、限于CA3且主要是单侧的投射——这是嵌入海马联想网络中的一个先前未被识别的回路模块。这种解剖和分子组织在哺乳动物（包括小鼠、猪和人类）中是保守的，人类组织中的膜片钳记录显示了每个PN亚型的保守功能特征。总之，这些发现从根本上修正了海马CA3回路图，揭示其作为两个高度不同的回路元件，并提供了分子工具来剖析每层对海马计算和认知的贡献。

## Abstract
Associational memory is critically dependent on the CA3 recurrent circuit, at the centre of the hippocampal system. While diversity amongst CA3 pyramidal neurons (PNs) has been long reported, the principles organising cell type identity and their consequences for circuit function remain unclear. Here, we demonstrate that deep-superficial is the primary axis of heterogeneity in CA3. PNs segregate into two molecularly distinct subtypes, superficial St18-, and deep St18+ neurons, with distinct anatomical, molecular, and functional properties. Using an St18-Cre mouse line, we reveal that these subtypes have divergent wiring patterns. Superficial neurons form the canonical broad autoassociative network across hemispheres and project to CA1, while deep St18+ PNs form a strikingly distinct, CA3-restricted, and predominantly unilateral projection -- a previously unrecognised circuit module embedded within the hippocampal associative network. This anatomical and molecular organisation is conserved across mammals, including mice, pigs, and humans, where patch-clamp recording in human tissue demonstrates conserved functional hallmarks of each PN subtype. Together, these findings fundamentally revise the hippocampal CA3 circuit map, revealing it as two highly distinct circuit elements and providing the molecular tools to dissect the contribution of each layer to hippocampal computation and cognition.
