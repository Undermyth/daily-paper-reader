## 摘要
人类认知的一个关键方面是能够利用我们对物理规律的知识来预测物理事件。这种能力是基于抽象过程，还是根植于身体与环境的互动，仍是一个悬而未决的争论。我们以变重力下的物理推理为模型系统，展示了人类的实时具身体验会改变其高级物理推理。具体而言，我们在计算机化的推理游戏中测试参与者，同时使用电流前庭刺激来干扰其重力信号。在需要关于地球重力推理的游戏中，与无刺激条件相比，参与者在刺激条件下表现更差且策略欠佳。然而，当游戏涉及变重力推理时，刺激的影响有所减弱。我们的发现表明，身体的物理体验会改变像推理这样的高级认知技能，暗示人类对世界的心理表征植根于可适应的物理机制。

## Abstract
A critical aspect of human cognition is the ability to use our knowledge about the laws of physics to make predictions about physical events. Whether this ability is based on abstract processes or is grounded in our body-environment interactions remains an open debate. We used physical reasoning under altered gravity as a model system to show that human real-time embodied experience modifies their high-level physical reasoning. Specifically, we tested participants in computerised reasoning games, while disrupting their gravitational signalling using Galvanic Vestibular Stimulation (GVS). Participants failed more and had suboptimal strategies under the GVS condition compared to no-GVS in games requiring reasoning about terrestrial gravity. However, the effects of GVS were reduced when the games included reasoning about altered gravity. Our findings demonstrate how the physical experience of the body shifts high-level cognitive skill as reasoning, suggesting that human mental representation of the world is grounded in adaptable physical mechanisms.
