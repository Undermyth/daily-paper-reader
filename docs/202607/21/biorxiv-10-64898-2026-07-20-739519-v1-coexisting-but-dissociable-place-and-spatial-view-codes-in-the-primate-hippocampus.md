## 摘要
在啮齿类动物中，海马活动经典地以位置锚定的位置细胞来描述，而非人灵长类研究则强调空间视角细胞，其活动取决于所观察的空间区域。这一对比引发了关于灵长类和啮齿类动物的空间编码是否在生物学上不同的长期争论。我们在猕猴导航两条视觉上不同的线性轨道并间歇性地做出依赖于背景的目标选择时，记录了它们海马中的单个单元。通过使用广义线性模型将位置编码与空间视角编码分离，我们在猕猴海马中识别出三个共存群体：位置细胞、视角细胞以及同时编码这两种变量的联合细胞。即使在考虑了视觉采样后，位置细胞仍普遍存在并表现出类似啮齿类的特性——局部放电、方向调谐和背景重映射，而视角细胞则稳定地编码异位视觉空间。群体解码在集合水平上证实了这一分离。因此，稳健的位置编码与视角编码在灵长类海马中共同存在，但仍可分离，这表明早期灵长类研究中位置样反应的明显稀缺反映了方法学和任务相关的差异，而非基本的物种级分歧。

## Abstract
In rodents, hippocampal activity is classically described in terms of position-anchored place cells, whereas nonhuman primate work has emphasized spatial view cells, in which activity depends on the region of space being viewed. This contrast has fueled a longstanding debate over whether primate and rodent spatial codes are biologically distinct. We recorded single units in the hippocampus of macaques as they navigated linear tracks in two visually distinct contexts, intermittently making context-dependent object choices. Using a generalized linear model to dissociate position coding from spatial view coding, we identified three coexisting populations in the macaque hippocampus: Position cells, View cells, and Conjunctive cells encoding both variables. Position cells were prevalent and showed rodent-like properties-localized firing, directional tuning, and contextual remapping-even after accounting for visual sampling, whereas View cells stably encoded allocentric visual space. Population decoding confirmed this dissociation at the ensemble level. Robust position codes thus coexist with, yet remain dissociable from, view codes in the primate hippocampus, suggesting that the apparent scarcity of place-like responses in earlier primate work reflects methodological and task-related differences rather than a fundamental species-level divergence.

- **巧妙分离混淆变量**：通过GLM将位置与视角在单神经元水平分离，避免了传统空间信息量（SI/VI）无法区分耦合变量的缺陷。
