## 摘要
表征漂移，即神经群体编码随时间的逐渐演变，已在单神经元水平上被广泛记录。然而，这种漂移是否在更大的皮层群体和空间尺度上有组织地发生仍不清楚。在此，我们在严格控制的感觉输入和行为条件下检查了全皮层群体动态。使用宽场钙成像，我们在小鼠执行基于触须的纹理辨别任务时，纵向记录了25个皮层区域的第2/3层（L2/3）或第5层（L5）的兴奋性活动。在稳定任务表现期间，连续五天追踪感觉诱发活动。群体活动模式在数天内在皮层和两个层中重新组织。这种重组遵循不同的层特异性模式：值得注意的是，L5反应在大多数皮层区域表现出广泛、单调的活动下降，而L2/3反应则表现出空间局限性和异质性变化，这些变化在感觉期最强烈。这些层差异延伸到刺激期之后，L5比L2/3表现出更长时间的时间参与。总之，这些发现表明表征漂移可以作为一个在皮层间协调且具有不同层特异性特征的过程展开。因此，漂移可能反映了全皮层回路动态的一个结构化特征。

## Abstract
Representational drift, the gradual evolution of neural population codes over days, has been widely documented at the level of single neurons. However, whether drift is organized across larger cortical populations and spatial scales remains unclear. Here, we examined cortex-wide population dynamics under tightly controlled sensory input and behavior. Using widefield calcium imaging, we longitudinally recorded excitatory activity in Layers 2/3 (L2/3) or Layer 5 (L5) across 25 cortical areas while mice performed a whisker-based texture discrimination task. Sensory-evoked activity was tracked over five consecutive days during stable task performance. Population activity patterns reorganized over days, across the cortex, in both layers. This reorganization followed distinct laminar motifs: notably L5 responses exhibited a widespread, monotonic decrease in activity across most cortical areas, whereas L2/3 responses showed spatially localized and heterogeneous changes that were strongest during the sensory period. These laminar differences extended beyond the stimulus period, with L5 exhibiting more prolonged temporal engagement than L2/3. Together, these findings indicate that representational drift can unfold as a process that is coordinated across cortex with distinct laminar profiles. Thus, drift may reflect a structured feature of cortex-wide circuit dynamics.
