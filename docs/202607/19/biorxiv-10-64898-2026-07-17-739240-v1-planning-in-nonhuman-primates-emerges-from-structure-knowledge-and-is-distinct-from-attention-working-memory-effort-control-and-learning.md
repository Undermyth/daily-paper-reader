## 摘要
前瞻性规划涉及对未来选择的心理模拟。这种心理模拟可能构成独立的认知操作，也可能与支持选择选项工作记忆、隐蔽注意信息采样、形成问题结构记忆模型以及追求未来目标的努力控制等经典认知操作紧密相关。我们通过对非人灵长类动物进行物体序列规划任务评估，并结合一系列更简单的认知任务测试，检验了规划的相对独立性。序列任务通过意外地遮蔽未来序列位置的物体，促使非人灵长类动物提前规划未来的序列选择。我们发现，六个被试中有五个能提前规划2-3个序数位置。规划深度由序列结构知识预测，但构成一个独特的认知因素，与序列记忆、物体记忆和学习效率不同。同样，规划与工作记忆容量、注意力、抑制控制和努力控制相分离。这些发现表明，前瞻性规划构成一个独立的构念，不能还原为其他领域通用认知操作，并在成功形成问题结构内部模型的非人灵长类动物中出现。

## Abstract
Forward planning involves mentally simulating future choices. Such mental simulation may constitute an independent cognitive operation, or it may be intricately linked to canonical cognitive operations supporting working memory of choice options, covert attentional information sampling, forming memory models of problem structure and effort control for pursuing future goals. Here, we test the relative independence of planning in non-human primates with an object-sequence planning task assessed together with an assay of simpler cognitive tasks. The sequence task unexpectedly masked objects at future serial positions probing NHPs to plan ahead serial future choices. We found that five of six subjects planned ahead 2-3 ordinal positions. Planning depth was predicted by knowledge of the serial structure but constituted a unique cognitive factor that was distinct from sequence memory, object memory, and learning efficacy. Similarly, planning was segregated from working memory capacity, attention, inhibitory control and effort control. These findings suggest that planning ahead constitutes a separate construct not reducible to other domain-general cognitive operations and emerges in NHPs that successfully form an internal model of the problem structure.
